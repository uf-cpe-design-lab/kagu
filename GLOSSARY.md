# Kagu

A mobile, gridfinity-drawer storage cabinet for the UF CpE design lab, holding electronic components, tools and fasteners.

## Language

### The cabinet

**Cabinet**:
The whole rolling storage unit, from casters to top.
_Avoid_: Cart, chest, unit

**Bay**:
An opening in the cabinet that holds a stack of drawers, an instrument shelf, or nothing.
_Avoid_: Compartment, section, slot

**Tool Wall**:
A side face of the cabinet used for hanging tools (e.g. clamps).
_Avoid_: Pegboard, side rack

### Drawers and gridfinity

**Drawer**:
A sliding container in a bay that holds one baseplate.
_Avoid_: Tray, box

**Baseplate**:
The gridfinity grid that sits in a drawer and locates bins.
_Avoid_: Grid, plate

**Bin**:
A gridfinity container that sits on a baseplate.
_Avoid_: Cup, tub, module

**Locked Drawer**:
A drawer whose contents stay visible but can only be reached by staff, through its viewing lid.
_Avoid_: Display drawer, secure drawer

**Viewing Lid**:
The clear, hinged, lockable cover over a locked drawer.
_Avoid_: Acrylic lid, cover

### Tops

**Top Interface**:
The standard mounting on the cabinet that every top module attaches to.
_Avoid_: Top mount, top rails

**Top Module**:
Any swappable top that attaches through the top interface (worktop, shelving, ...).
_Avoid_: Top, lid, topper

### Fabrication

**Part Label**:
The letter code marked on every fabricated part that identifies it and its orientation during assembly.
_Avoid_: Part number, tag
